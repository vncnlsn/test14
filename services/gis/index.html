01 / DATA

Reconciliation as work changes

Surface and mineral interests do not always follow the same boundaries. Historical legal descriptions may not align with current parcels, and a route change can introduce a different set of affected properties.

APS reconciles those differences across project mapping and supporting data as work develops.
