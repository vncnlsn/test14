01 / OWNERSHIP

Records and research

Surface and mineral estates may have separated generations ago. Interests may be divided among heirs, and federal, state, or tribal lands can involve separate records and requirements.

APS traces and reconciles chains of title through county and parish courthouses, government land systems, recorded instruments, and historical archives.

02 / SCOPE

Title and leasing services

  • Full title search to sovereignty
  • Surface and mineral ownership
  • Probate, heirship, and fractional interests
  • Title curative
  • Lease research, negotiation, and acquisition
