SERVICES / 01

Seismic
Permitting

Land access and permitting for 2D, 3D, and 4D seismic surveys, microseismic, VSP programs, and other complex field projects.

01 / LAND ACCESS

Property conditions

Every property presents different conditions. Access routes, crops, livestock, hunting seasons, fences, water wells, existing infrastructure, environmental restrictions, compensation, and potential damages can affect field operations.

APS contacts landowners personally, negotiates and documents conditions, and oversees field compliance with the agreed requirements.

02 / PERMITS

Approvals and administration

APS coordinates applicable municipal, county, state, and federal approvals. Permit status is tracked and reported throughout the program.

Work also includes landowner payments, check processing, required IRS filings, and surface damage settlement coordination.

03 / PREPARATION

Early involvement

Preliminary ownership research can reveal divided interests, access difficulties, and the parties whose agreements may be required before significant resources are committed.
