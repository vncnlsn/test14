About APS
Land Services

A land-services company serving energy, exploration, and infrastructure projects across North America.

01 / EXPERIENCE

Built on field experience

APS reports hundreds of energy and infrastructure projects and thousands of negotiations involving permitting, land access, right-of-way, leasing, and other property rights.

Project experience spans 28 states, Alberta, and Saskatchewan, including private, state, federal, and tribal lands. APS reports locations in Utah, Montana, and Texas.
