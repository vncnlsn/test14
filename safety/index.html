Health, safety, and environmental compliance and accident prevention are part of APS project responsibilities.

01 / PRACTICES

Field safety

APS work follows its Safety Program Manual, Injury and Illness Prevention Program (IIPP), and applicable client and project requirements.

Personnel participate in daily safety briefings and job safety analyses (JSAs), monitor field operations for compliance, and exercise stop-work authority when conditions fall outside established requirements.

02 / CONTRACTOR MANAGEMENT

ISNetworld membership

APS Land Services is a member of ISNetworld. For safety-program or contractor-management questions, contact APS leadership.
